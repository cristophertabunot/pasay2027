Traffic and getting around on foot

Do

  • Use Grab or InDrive instead of flagging a taxi on the street
  • Use a footbridge to cross, SM Mall of Asia has several along Macapagal Blvd and Seaside Blvd
  • Build in extra time for weekend evening trips around SM Mall of Asia and Roxas Blvd

Don't

  • Don't assume a driver will stop for you at a marked crossing
  • Don't cross without a full, clear gap in traffic
  • Don't jaywalk, Metro Manila's Anti-Jaywalking Ordinance carries a ₱500 fine, and Pasay is one of the cities that actively enforces it

Watch out for

  • Traffic keeps to the right, same as most of Southeast Asia
  • Mall and casino traffic gets heavy around SM Mall of Asia and Roxas Blvd on weekend evenings
  • Smoking is only allowed in designated areas under the national Tobacco Regulation Act, not just mall policy, malls and Okada enforce this by law

First-timer's guide

Sourced from SM Mall of Asia Arena's official advisory: sm-arena.com/MOA/advisory

Do

  • Arrive early, gates open 3 hours before the show
  • Stay in your assigned seat for the whole show
  • Take souvenir photos with a regular phone or camera
  • Buy tickets only from Ticketmaster PH or SM Tickets to avoid counterfeits
  • Cheer, sing, and dance, just stay respectful of the fans around you

Don't

  • Don't bring a bag bigger than 12 x 12 inches
  • Don't bring outside food or drinks
  • Don't bring a professional camera, tripod, or selfie stick
  • Don't record or livestream the show
  • Don't leave the venue expecting to re-enter, it's a strict no re-entry policy
